All releases of SereS

v1.3.0-beta

21.10.2026 Latest Beta
Added
  • Right-click a message (long-press on phones) to edit, delete or see its info
  • Message info: when a message was sent, delivered and seen by each member (on phones: swipe the message to the left)
  • See when someone is typing: in the chat header, the chat list and as an animated bubble above the input field
  • Ticks on your messages: ✓ sent, ✓✓ delivered, blue ✓✓ seen
  • Edited messages are marked as edited
  • Reply to messages (menu, double-click or swipe right on phones) with a quote that jumps to the original
  • Reactions with emoji: quick reactions in the message menu and a full emoji picker
  • Send photos, videos and files (up to 16 MB): end-to-end encrypted, with an image viewer, drag & drop and pasting
  • Voice messages: record with the microphone button
  • Text formatting like Discord: **bold**, *italic*, ~~strike~~, `code`, ```code blocks```, ||spoilers|| and > quotes
  • @mentions in groups with suggestions; messages that mention you are highlighted
  • Forward messages and attachments to other chats
  • Copy the text of a message
  • Pin important messages in a chat and see them at the top
  • Search inside a chat
  • Disappearing messages: 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days
  • Pin chats to the top of the list and archive chats
  • Drafts: unsent text stays in the chat when you switch
  • Online status and "last seen", can be hidden in the settings
  • Desktop notifications for new messages (optional)
  • Button to jump to the newest message, mark chats as read, edit your last message with the arrow-up key
  • 10 backup codes (like Discord): shown once after registering or in the settings, download as .txt; reset your password with a code and keep all your encrypted chats
  • "Forgot password?" on the login page: with a backup code or by e-mail (by e-mail you can't read your old messages anymore)
  • Two-factor authentication (optional, in the settings): use your phone or a security key as passkey, or an authenticator app; backup codes work if you lose your device
Changes
  • Deleted messages disappear completely, without a trace
  • One "New message" button: pick one friend for a direct chat or several for a group chat

v1.2.0-beta

10.10.2026 Beta
Added
  • SereS is now available in English, German and French
  • Change the language on the login page or in the settings
  • Cookie banner: choose whether SereS may remember your login, language and design
  • Cookie settings in the settings menu to change your choice later
  • Display names: choose a name in the settings, it is shown everywhere instead of your username
  • Profiles: right-click a chat or friend (or click an avatar or name) to see the profile with the @username
  • Show the security code, mute or block someone right from their profile
  • Muted people are marked with 🔕 and don't count as unread anymore
  • The tab icon shows how many unread messages you have
  • Invite link and QR code: others can open or scan it to send you a friend request
  • Share, copy or renew your invite link (renewing turns off the old link and QR code)
  • Right-click a chat to delete it, or a group to leave it (long-press on phones)
Changes
  • English is the default language
  • Without cookie consent you are logged out when you close the browser
  • The encryption notice is now only shown inside chats
  • Dates and times are shown in the format of your language
  • Blocked people are listed under Friends and can be unblocked there
  • Unblocking a former friend makes you friends again
  • New mobile design: dialogs and menus slide up from the bottom, bigger buttons
  • The chat fits the visible screen on phones (address bar, notch and home bar)
  • Typing on iPhones no longer zooms into the page

v1.1.0-beta

08.10.2026 Beta
Added
  • End-to-end encryption for all new messages (the server only stores ciphertext)
  • Security codes to verify your contacts, warning when a key changes
  • Group chats: create, rename, add & remove members, leave
  • Friends tab: remove friends, block & unblock users, cancel sent requests
  • Unread counters, date separators, delete messages for everyone, clickable links
  • Change password (your keys are re-encrypted on your device)
  • Profile pictures of your friends
Changes
  • Deleting a direct chat now only removes it for you
  • Messages update every few seconds instead of every 10 seconds
  • Old accounts and chats are migrated automatically on the first login
  • Security: HttpOnly session cookies, rate limiting, Content-Security-Policy, XSS fixes
